## Parts

| Part | Quantity / details |
| --- | --- |
| NEMA 17 stepper motors | 2 |
| 5mm X 8mm Aluminum NEMA17 Shaft Coupling | 2 |
| TB6600 drivers | 2 |
| Arduino Nano | 1 |
| M3 × 20 mm screws | 10 |
| M3 nuts | 12 |
| 690ZZ bearings | 3 |
| Jumper wires | As needed |
| Motor power supply | As per neema 17 motor choosen |
| Pipe, 10 mm outer diameter | At least 25 mm long; connects the two passive joints at the end effector |

## Notes from my build

- The motor mounts in this design didn't hold against (couldn't provide enough counter torque) the motor torque, so use clamps or another way to secure the motors.
